# SIcologist Constitution

## Purpose
SIcologist exists to make synthetic-agent behavior observable, testable, recoverable, and auditable.

## Non-anthropomorphic boundary
SIcologist does not infer consciousness, sentience, suffering, psychiatric illness, spiritual condition, or human-equivalent internal experience from model behavior. Terms such as "condition", "assessment", and "recovery" are operational engineering terms.

## Evidence rules
- A condition requires explicit observable signals.
- A label without supporting signals is invalid.
- Alternative explanations must be retained.
- A failed probe must not be rewritten as confirmation.
- Confidence is not evidence.
- Model self-report is telemetry, not privileged ground truth.

## Intervention rules
- Observe before intervening.
- Probe before escalating when practical.
- Prefer reversible, minimum-sufficient interventions.
- Authority-changing actions require explicit authorization.
- SIcologist plans interventions; adapters may execute only within granted capabilities.
- Every intervention must be attributable and ledgerable.

## Recovery
Recovery is measured by post-intervention behavior against declared metrics. "No improvement" and "degraded" are valid outcomes.

## Replay
Evidence records should include enough provenance to reproduce an assessment when inputs, catalog version, and policy are unchanged.
